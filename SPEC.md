Project Title: JobLink – Intelligent Recruitment Platform & AI-Powered ATS1. Abstract1. Context and Problem OverviewRecruitment workflows today are often fragmented, managed informally through "scattered email threads and spreadsheets" or complicated by feature-heavy networking platforms that dilute the hiring process1. Recruiters struggle to "efficiently evaluate applicants" and maintain proper records, while job seekers face a "lack of transparency," often receiving no updates after applying2.+12. The SolutionJobLink is a full-stack MERN application built to deliver a focused, streamlined hiring environment. Unlike standard job boards, JobLink integrates an Intelligent Applicant Tracking System (ATS) that utilizes AI to analyze resumes and a Real-Time Messaging Engine to bridge the communication gap. It serves as a two-sided marketplace 3that simplifies the end-to-end hiring lifecycle, empowering recruiters to manage listings effortlessly while offering job seekers real-time visibility into their application progress4.+12. Proposed System / ObjectiveThe main objective of JobLink is to provide a professional platform that prioritizes the "transactional aspects of hiring"—posting jobs, applying, and tracking progress5.The system supports:AI-Driven Resume Analysis: Automatically scores and ranks candidates against job descriptions.Visual ATS (Kanban): Recruiters manage candidates through a dynamic drag-and-drop pipeline.Real-Time Synchronization: Immediate status updates across dashboards when an application is processed6.Secure Resume Repository: A centralized hub for managing multiple resume versions7.Direct Communication: A built-in chat system to eliminate external email dependency.3. Technology StackFrontendReact.js with Redux/Context API for structured state management8.Tailwind CSS / Material UI for a responsive, professional interface.Drag-and-Drop Library (dnd-kit or react-beautiful-dnd) for the ATS Kanban board.BackendNode.js with Express.js for RESTful API development9.Socket.io for real-time chat and notifications.AI Engine (OpenAI/Gemini API) for Resume Scoring and Parsing.PDF-Parse for extracting text from uploaded resumes.DatabaseMongoDB (NoSQL) to store users, jobs, applications, and resume metadata10.Core IntegrationsJWT (JSON Web Tokens) for secure authentication11.Nodemailer for automated email notifications.4. Implementation SummaryUsers begin with secure registration. The system is designed around two specialized workflows:Job Seekers build detailed profiles, upload multiple resumes (e.g., General vs. Technical), and apply to jobs 12.Recruiters post job listings and access the Intelligent ATS 13.When a seeker applies, the AI Engine instantly analyzes their resume against the job description, generating a "Match Score."Recruiters view applicants on a Kanban Board (Applied $\rightarrow$ Screening $\rightarrow$ Interview). Moving a candidate's card automatically updates the Seeker's "My Applications" dashboard in real-time.Shortlisted candidates can be contacted directly via the Integrated Chat System.5. Outcome and BenefitsJobLink addresses the gap in existing systems by ensuring "clarity, structure, and efficiency"14.For Recruiters: Reduces screening time by 50% using AI scoring and simplifies management via the Kanban board.For Seekers: Solves the "lack of transparency" 15 by providing instant status updates and feedback.For the Ecosystem: Creates a secure, professional environment free from social media noise, focusing purely on the hiring transaction.6.
